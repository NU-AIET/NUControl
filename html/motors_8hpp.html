Detailed Description

Motor electrical parameter structs and pre-defined motor instances.

MotorParameters aggregates all per-motor constants needed by the FOC controller. Pre-defined instances are provided for the motors used in the NUControl exoskeleton platform.

kT / kV relationship

For a sinusoidal BLDC motor in the amplitude-invariant Park frame:

\[ k_T \approx \frac{1}{k_V \cdot \sqrt{3/2}} \]

The U2523/U2535 entries have an unusually small kV (0.001 V·s/rad)

MotorParameters U2535 {7, 0.72487f, 510.f * 1e-6f, 3.f, 9.f, 0.040f, 0.001f}

T-Motor U2535 BLDC motor parameters.

High inductance (510 µH) results in relatively slow electrical dynamics (RL time constant τ = L/R ≈ 700 µs ≈ 7 control steps at 10 kHz).

Note
kV = 0.001 V·s/rad is very small; the back-EMF contribution is negligible at typical operating speeds (<30 rad/s mechanical).
