Detailed Description

template<typename T>
struct RingBuffer< T >

Fixed-capacity circular shift register backed by a pre-allocated vector.

Replaces std::deque in DiscreteFilter shift registers to eliminate heap allocation from the ISR hot path. The capacity is fixed at construction; push() overwrites the oldest slot in O(1) without any heap operations.

Indexing is by age: [0] is the most recently pushed value, [1] is one sample older, etc.
