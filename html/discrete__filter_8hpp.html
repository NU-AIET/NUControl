Detailed Description

General discrete-time IIR/FIR filter, Butterworth 2nd-order, PI controller, and motor feedforward filter implementations.

All filters share the DiscreteFilter<T,G> base class which implements the standard difference equation:

\[ y[n] = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} b_k\, x[n-k] - \sum_{j=0}^{M-1} a_j\, y[n-j-1] \]

where \( b_k \) are the feedforward (input / numerator) coefficients and \( a_j \) are the feedback (output / denominator) coefficients. The sign convention matches standard direct-form II transposed (i.e., feedback coefficients are subtracted).

Specialisations:

  • Butterworth2nd<T> — 2nd-order Butterworth low-pass (bilinear transform)
  • PIController<T> — Tustin-discretised PI controller
  • MotorFeedforward — bilinear RL plant inversion for feedforward current control
